# User guide

IP Messenger is a chat app for direct and group conversations. This guide covers everything you can do in it.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up.** Open the app and choose **Sign up**. Enter a display name (what others see), your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
2. **Confirm your email,** if the app asks you to. Click the link in the email and you're signed in.
3. **Sign in** later with your email and password.
4. **Forgot your password?** On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?** and enter your email. Open the link in the email (in the same browser), choose a new password, and you're signed in. The link works once and expires after an hour.

## Finding your way around

- **On a computer:** the sidebar on the left has the navigation (**Chats, Contacts, Profile, Settings**), your conversations, and your name with **Sign out** at the bottom. The open chat fills the rest of the window.
- **On a phone:** you see one screen at a time, with tabs at the bottom. Inside a chat the tabs are hidden. Use **←** to go back to your chats.

## Installing the app

IP Messenger can be installed like a regular app, with its own icon and window:

- **Android (Chrome):** open the menu **⋮** and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** tap **Share** and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome, Edge):** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

The installed app is the same as the website and updates by itself. Without a connection it shows a "You're offline" page and reloads once you're back online.

## Starting a conversation

1. Go to **Contacts**. Everyone with an account is listed; type a name and press **Search** to narrow the list.
2. Next to the person, press **Message**. You'll always get the same chat with that person; it never creates duplicates.

A green dot on someone's picture means they're online right now.

## Chatting

- **Send:** type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** or **Send**. Use **Shift + Enter** for a new line. Messages can be up to 4,000 characters.
- **Sending status:** your message appears immediately with "Sending…" until it's saved. If it shows **Not sent**, check your connection and send it again.
- **Live updates:** new messages from others appear by themselves; you don't need to refresh.
- **Messages:** they're shown oldest to newest, with the date between days. In group chats, each message shows who sent it.
- **Online status:** in a one-to-one chat, the header shows **Online** or when the person was **last seen**.

## Your chats list

Conversations are sorted by the latest activity, with a preview of the last message ("You: …" for your own). A **blue number** shows how many messages you haven't read. It clears when you open the chat.

## Profile

Under **Profile** you can:
- change your **display name** (1–50 characters) and **status message** (up to 140 characters, shown to others)
- **upload a picture** (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB), change it, or remove it to go back to your initials
- see **Your connection (IP)**: the public IP address the app sees for you. Only you can see it; it's not saved or shown to anyone else. Details: [network-information.md](network-information.md)

## Settings

Under **Settings**:
- **Theme:** Light, Dark, or **Match my device**, which follows your computer or phone's setting.
- **Notifications:**
  - **Notify me about new messages** shows a system notification when a message arrives in a chat you aren't looking at. Click it to open the chat. Each device has to allow notifications once: choose **Allow notifications** under the setting (or allow them when your browser asks). If they're blocked, allow them in your browser's site settings.
  - **Play a sound for new messages** plays a short chime. Browsers only play sound after you've clicked somewhere in the app once.

Settings are saved to your account, so they follow you to every device. Notifications work while the app is open, in a tab or window, even in the background; the desktop app shows them as normal system notifications.

## Privacy

- Only the members of a conversation can read it or post in it.
- Other users see your display name, status, picture, online status and last-seen time. They never see your email or IP address.

## If something goes wrong

If a page says **Something went wrong**, press **Try again**. If it keeps happening, send the **Error ID** shown on the page to whoever runs the app. It lets them find exactly what happened.
